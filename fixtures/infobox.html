Overview

Harbor Tower is an office building overlooking the central waterfront. The building was completed in 1985 after four years of construction.

Its observation level occupies the upper floors, above a public lobby and a small exhibition about the harbor.

Design

The structure uses a tapered stone base and a steel frame. Repeating vertical windows reduce glare while keeping the interior bright.
